# Fonts

**Space Grotesk** (digits and a few symbols only) is embedded in `styles.css`
for the numbers on the home dashboard. It is licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org), not the MIT license
that covers the rest of this repository.

The Damascene theme's own typeface, *itf Qomra Arabic*, lives in the
[theme repository](https://github.com/k7aleds8/damascene) and is not part of this plugin.
